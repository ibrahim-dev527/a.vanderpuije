OUR STORY

The A.Vanderpuije Story

A.Vanderpuije Clothing is a fashion destination in Accra focused on African inspired ready to wear style. The brand brings distinctive prints and contemporary fashion together for customers looking for pieces with character.

FROM THE STOREFRONT

Fashion built around the fabric.

At A.Vanderpuije Clothing, the prints lead the design. Each piece is chosen and styled to let the fabric's colour and pattern take centre stage, paired with silhouettes made for everyday wear.

Customers can browse in person at either Accra location, or reach out directly on WhatsApp to ask about a specific piece.
